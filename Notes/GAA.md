# Section 1 - Relevance
My research was based in the cybersecurity department at the National Labratory of the Rockies (NLR).  Our goal was to detect malicious users attempting to breach celluar networks.  Hackers will overwhelm culluar towers, which can lead to a poor signal for mobile phones or other technology.  We wanted to find a method to find these hackers automatically.  I was assigned to build models to find and stop these hackers. 

# Section 2 - Methods
My models are essentially instructions built on math.  We take data about the people connected to the celluar towers and ask the model whether it is likely to be legitamte users or malicious users.  We have an AI model that controls all of these smaller models and acts like a supervisor.  If one of the small models detects a malicious user, the AI decides what to do with that malicious user.  My mentor in this program designed an isolated environment that the AI will re-direct the malicious user to.  It essentially puts this malicious user in a fake celluar tower.  This allows us to monitor and control what they can do without them being able to affect the real celluar towers.  It also prevents them from knowing that we have re-directed and stopped their threat.  From their perspective, they are still in the real celluar tower.

Each celluar tower is called a node, and each node has multiple cells.  Each cell has its own group of people that are connected to it, or users.  My approach is a three tiered structure: the bottom tier is the basic model that decides if there is a threat or not.  This is at the cell level.  At the node level, there is a model which supervises all of the cells.  It takes data from each cell and directs each cell's model how to act.  It decides how aggresive the cell model's should be in detecting threats.  Lastly, there is the AI agent at the top.  This AI supervises multiple nodes, or celluar towers, and its job is to decide what to do when a threat is detected.  It actually steers the malicious user away from the real celluar towers and into the fake tower.
